What to Expect

Conversational.
Not a panel. Not a keynote.

The interview runs 20–25 minutes. Dr. Joe drives with short, direct questions. You do most of the talking. There are no gotcha moments, but there are no easy questions either. Expect to be challenged on your data, your decisions, and your vision.

For Invited Guests

How the Conversation Flows

01

Opening

Dr. Joe opens with your specific data: the numbers that look impossible on paper. Expect a direct challenge: "How are you actually doing this?"

02

Your Edge

What personal wiring or unconventional background gives you an advantage others don't have? He connects your story to your outcomes.

03

The Evidence

Dr. Joe plays skeptic. He'll present ways someone could explain away your results, then ask you to prove him wrong. Specifics win here.

04

Your Story

The origin moment. When did you know this work was yours? Who believed in you before the results arrived? This is where the show gets human.

05

The Model

How do you operationalize your philosophy? Name the programs, partners, systems, and people. He will push past principles every time.

06

The Future

Where are you taking this? Dr. Joe will ask whether you're building something with national implications, and whether you're ready to own that.

Prepare to Win

How to Prepare

Five things to have ready before the conversation starts.

01

Know your numbers.

Dr. Joe will already have them. The more specifically you can speak to your data, the better the conversation.

02

Own your origin story.

When did you choose this work? Who believed in you before the results were there?

03

Name your contrarian move.

What did you do that most leaders in your seat wouldn't? Be ready to own it.

04

Don't avoid your challenge.

Dr. Joe won't skip the hard part. Tension is what makes the episode worth watching.

05

Bring specifics.

Program names, partners, timelines, outcomes. He'll push past philosophy every time.
