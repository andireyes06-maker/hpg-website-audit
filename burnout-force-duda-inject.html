Force No. 01

Fragmentation. The Silo Tax.

“I am not safe unless I am in control.”

Every silo in your organization started as someone protecting something real: a budget, a decision, a team. But protection that never opens back up becomes control that isolates everyone else. Fragmentation doesn't look like conflict. It looks like everyone technically doing their job, and nothing ever quite lining up.

The Antidote
The Launch Check
Before any initiative launches, answer publicly: what problem are we solving, who needs to know this exists, and what breaks if we don't coordinate here.
