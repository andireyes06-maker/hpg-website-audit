Who This Is For

Designed for leaders who
still ask questions.

The Peer-2-Peer Roundtable is built on one premise: K–12 and higher education have more to learn from each other than from staying in their own lane. The only credential that earns a seat is a genuine willingness to learn across sectors.

K-12 Leadership

Superintendents & Assistant Superintendents

You navigate board politics, legislative pressure, community trust, and a workforce facing historic burnout, all while holding a coherent strategic vision together. You deserve peers who understand the full weight of that chair.

Higher Education

Presidents, Chancellors & Provosts

You're managing enrollment headwinds, faculty-administration tensions, and a rapidly shifting value proposition for your institution. The best thinking on your most pressing problems isn't always coming from within your sector, and you know it.

✓  This is for you if…
  • ✓ You lead K–12 or higher ed facing real relevance and financial viability pressures
  • ✓ You suspect peer collaboration across sectors beats staying in your lane
  • ✓ You're willing to be challenged by a university president or superintendent who solved your exact problem differently
  • ✓ You want authentic dialogue, not another space for institutional optics management
  • ✓ You're comfortable sitting with questions that don't have clean answers
✗  This is NOT for you if…
  • ✗ You're not genuinely concerned about your institution's relevance or financial trajectory
  • ✗ You're convinced your sector already has the best answers
  • ✗ You're unwilling to question the assumptions your peer group has always shared
  • ✗ You want a panel where leaders promote their institution's programs and initiatives
  • ✗ You're looking for the already-learned. Only active learners welcome
The Format

What happens in
the room.

01

Cross-Sector Peer Discussion

Superintendents and college presidents at the same table, learning how the other sector frames and solves shared challenges. The cross-sector lens is the point, not a side benefit.

02

No Pitches. No Vendors.

This is not a webinar. There's no product pitch, no sponsor slot, no presenter promoting their institution. Just executives who agreed to show up and think out loud together.

03

Topic-Driven, Peer-Led

Each session centers on a challenge that's live for both K–12 and higher ed. The brief goes out in advance. The conversation follows the people in the room, not a slide deck.
